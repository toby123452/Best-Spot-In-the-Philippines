Introduction

Palawan and the surrounding Philippine islands are world-renowned for their dramatic limestone cliffs, crystal-clear emerald waters, and vibrant marine life. Here are our top featured destinations.

Puerto Princesa Underground River

This UNESCO World Heritage Site features a navigable underground river that flows directly into the sea, winding through a magnificent limestone cave filled with breathtaking stalactites and stalagmites.

El Nido's Big Lagoon

Famous for its dramatic black limestone cliffs rising sharply out of shallow turquoise waters, El Nido offers world-class kayaking, hidden lagoons, and vibrant coral reefs ideal for snorkeling.

Kayangan Lake, Coron

Located in Coron, this majestic freshwater lake is framed by rugged rock formations and offers crystal-clear visibility, making it one of the most photographed and tranquil spots in the country.
